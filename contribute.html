OPEN-SOURCE PROCESS

Help make the curriculum better.

The project wants criticism more than applause. Report what is wrong, confusing, unsafe, or hard to teach.

Content Error

Flag a factual, technical, safety, or attribution problem. Never include wallet secrets or personal financial information.

Confusing Section

Tell us where wording, jargon, an example, or an exercise stopped making sense.

Human Review

Bitcoin technical reviewers and adult educators are invited to critique narrow slices of the curriculum.

No manufactured social proof: reviewers are asked for corrections, not endorsements. A person is not publicly listed as a supporter or reference without permission.
