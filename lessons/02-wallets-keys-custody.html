Wallets, Keys, Recovery Phrases & Custody

Learner target: Distinguish an exchange account from self-custody and understand why recovery information is sensitive.

Education only. No real-money exercise is required. Never enter a real recovery phrase, private key, wallet password, or exchange login into a lesson or form.

Key ideas

  • A wallet application helps you interact with keys and the Bitcoin network.
  • A private key is secret spending authority.
  • A recovery phrase can restore access to many common wallet setups and must be protected.
  • Anyone who obtains a usable recovery phrase or private key may be able to take the bitcoin controlled by those keys.
  • Losing recovery information with no other usable backup can mean permanent loss of access.
  • Never type a real recovery phrase into a website, chat, support form, cloud note, or email; do not photograph it for convenience.
  • An exchange account can show a bitcoin balance while the exchange controls the keys.
  • A Bitcoin address or transaction ID is not a password, but publishing it can reveal transaction history and other financial information.

Example

A fake support agent says, “Send me your 12 words and I’ll restore your wallet.” The correct response is to stop. A support agent does not need your recovery phrase.

Try it

Classify these as PRIVATE or DEPENDS: Bitcoin address, private key, recovery phrase, transaction ID, wallet balance.
