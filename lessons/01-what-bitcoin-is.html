What Bitcoin Is

Learner target: Explain Bitcoin without defining it only as an investment or something that goes up.

Education only. No real-money exercise is required. Never enter a real recovery phrase, private key, wallet password, or exchange login into a lesson or form.

Key ideas

  • Bitcoin is a digital monetary network and asset whose rules are enforced by software and network participants rather than one central company.
  • Bitcoin can be transferred over the network without requiring one central payment operator to approve every transaction.
  • Control ultimately depends on control of the information needed to authorize spending.
  • Price is a market outcome, not the definition of Bitcoin.

Example

A learner says, “Bitcoin is like a company stock on the internet.” The correction is simple: Bitcoin is not a company and does not have a CEO who can change its monetary rules by corporate decision.

Try it

In two or three sentences: What is Bitcoin, and what is one thing it is not?
