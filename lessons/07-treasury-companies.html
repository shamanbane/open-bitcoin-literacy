Bitcoin Treasury Companies

Learner target: Separate corporate bitcoin exposure from personally controlled bitcoin.

Education only. No real-money exercise is required. Never enter a real recovery phrase, private key, wallet password, or exchange login into a lesson or form.

Key ideas

  • A Bitcoin treasury company is a company that holds bitcoin as part of its corporate treasury or capital strategy.
  • A shareholder owns a security or equity claim, not the company’s Bitcoin private keys.
  • Company value can be affected by debt, shares, dilution, operations, management, regulation, market sentiment, and other claims — not only Bitcoin’s spot price.
  • Buying a company’s stock is not self-custody.

Example

A company owns bitcoin, borrows money, issues shares, pays employees, and makes business decisions. A shareholder owns part of that company — not the company’s Bitcoin private keys.

Try it

Complete these two sentences: “Bitcoin in my self-custody wallet means …” and “Stock in a company that owns bitcoin means …”
