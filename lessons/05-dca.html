Dollar-Cost Averaging as Illustrative Math

Learner target: Define DCA without treating it as a guaranteed investment strategy.

Education only. No real-money exercise is required. Never enter a real recovery phrase, private key, wallet password, or exchange login into a lesson or form.

Key ideas

  • DCA means making equal or planned purchases or contributions on a schedule rather than choosing one single purchase time.
  • The amount of bitcoin received can vary with price.
  • DCA does not guarantee profit, remove risk, or make an asset appropriate for a person.

Example

A fictional learner buys $25 of bitcoin every Friday for four weeks. Total contributed = $100; the amount of bitcoin received can differ each week.

Try it

A fictional learner contributes $20 each week for five weeks. How much cash was contributed? Does that alone tell you whether the learner made money?

Pilot note: Formal worksheet scoring happens in the in-person Pilot 1 facilitator pack, not on this public page.
