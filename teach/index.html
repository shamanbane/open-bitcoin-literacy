Opening disclosure

“Today is a Bitcoin literacy class. I personally invest in bitcoin-related assets and I build a separate investment-research project. Nothing in today’s class is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold bitcoin or any security. I will not discuss my personal holdings or give opinions about specific assets during this session.”

Assessment procedure

  1. PRE is taken on paper.
  2. Learners do not see PRE scores during the session.
  3. PRE papers are collected face-down.
  4. Worksheets are used during lessons.
  5. Before POST, worksheets are put away and collected face-down.
  6. POST is taken on paper.
  7. Scoring happens after the session using separate facilitator keys.
