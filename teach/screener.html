PILOT RECRUITMENT

Beginner Screener

Use at signup only. Do not link screener answers to learner codes or assessment results.

  1. Have you personally set up a self-custody Bitcoin wallet and written down a recovery phrase?
  2. Could you explain what a Bitcoin transaction confirmation means without looking it up?
  3. Have you personally sent bitcoin from a self-custody wallet to another Bitcoin address?
  4. Do you currently work, teach, or regularly contribute in Bitcoin as a technical/education field?
Priority beginner fit: No / Not sure on at least 2 of questions 1–3 and No on question 4.
