Practical-Task Rubric

This page summarizes the scoring rules from the canonical Pilot V1.2.2 facilitator pack.

Lesson 1

PASS if the learner describes Bitcoin as a digital monetary network/asset or peer-to-peer money system and names one thing it is not.

Lesson 2

Private key and recovery phrase must be PRIVATE. Address and transaction ID are DEPENDS because they are shareable but can expose financial history.

Lesson 3

Verification and re-check must happen before authorization. Authorization before verification = NEEDS REVISION.

Lesson 4

Recovery-phrase request, guaranteed return, and urgent buy-now pressure = RED FLAG. A public transaction ID is not automatically a red flag.

Lesson 5

$20 × 5 = $100 and that alone does not tell whether profit occurred.

Lesson 6

PASS requires understanding growth on the updated base and that assumed-rate calculators do not guarantee real returns.

Lesson 7

PASS requires direct-key-control vs company-security distinction plus one company-specific factor.

Paper worksheets are collected face-down before POST, scored after the session, transferred to the internal metrics sheet, and destroyed on the Pilot 1 retention schedule.
