PILOT 1

Rules That Protect the Learner

  • Adults age 18+ only.
  • In-person only for Pilot 1.
  • No real-money exercise.
  • No account opening requirement.
  • No real seed phrase, private key, wallet password, exchange login, or wallet balance collection.
  • Paper PRE/POST is primary.
  • HTML assessment remains facilitator-only during Pilot 1.
  • No participant photos, audio, or video without separate written media consent.
  • No facilitator holdings talk, price targets, allocation advice, or named-asset opinions during class.
Data handling: consent sheets stay separate from learner codes. No identity↔code lookup table is kept.
