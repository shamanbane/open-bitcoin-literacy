ABOUT THE PROJECT

Education first. Evidence before scale.

Open Bitcoin Literacy is a free, open-source, non-advisory education project designed to help ordinary adults understand Bitcoin in plain language.

What makes this project different

The long-term lane is not only “what is Bitcoin?” It also includes how people, companies, and institutions use Bitcoin — especially Bitcoin treasury-company and public-disclosure literacy — while keeping education separate from investment advice.

AI use

AI tools assist with drafting, organization, editing, and research workflow. AI output is not treated as an authority or human reviewer. External corrections are tracked, and the project owner remains responsible for what is published.

Open-source posture

Curriculum/content is intended to follow the project’s CC BY-SA 4.0 posture; educational code follows the project’s MIT posture. Brand marks are not automatically licensed by those content/code licenses.

Relationship to Treasury Sentinel

Open Bitcoin Literacy is being separated as its own education surface. The beginner path should not funnel learners into tickers, mNAV, Sentinel Pro, price targets, or named-asset research.
